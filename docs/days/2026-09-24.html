How to read this page

Fair odds = 1 ÷ model probability. Best odds = highest UK price (bookmakers and Betfair Exchange). Exchange prices are compared after commission (shown as net). Edge = model probability × best odds (after any commission) − 1. Market = the bookmakers' average price with their margin removed. Stake = 0.25 Kelly as % of bankroll, for guidance only. xG = model's expected goals, home–away. The model knows nothing about injuries, suspensions or rotation.
